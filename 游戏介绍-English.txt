■ About
LULLABY MANOR is a 3D paper-storybook dream rescue. At midnight the little
music box in the attic begins to play on its own. One by one the Marlowe
family — Ollie, Dad, Biscuit the dog, Mom — fall asleep where they stand and
do not wake. You are June. You wake as a small light with a lantern. Step
into five nightmares, gather three lost memories in each, and bring your
family home before dawn.

Every dream has its own rule. In the nursery, the Hushlings hunt by sound.
In the drowned study, the tide wakes the ink eels. In the thorn garden,
hounds charge in straight lines and briar gates cut the path. In the
clockwork attic, pendulums and sentinels move to a tick. In the mirror
gallery, your shadow copies where you have been. No timers, no stamina —
just observation, quiet routes, a dream ability, and one more staircase
toward the attic.

Everything you see and hear was made for this game: cardboard-and-felt
dioramas, GLSL jelly characters, procedurally built enemies and a boss, and
an original soundtrack. No ripped assets, no downloads, no account. Open a
browser and play.

■ How to play
1. Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Hold Shift to tiptoe and shrink the
   range at which nightmares can hear you.
2. Find the three memories in each stage — lullaby notes, lost pages,
   moonbones, brass gears or mirror shards. The exit door wakes only when
   all three are yours.
3. Step into the glowing door to clear the stage. If a memory is missing,
   the door tells you how many are left.
4. A nightmare hit spends a guard spark first, then a heart. Lose all three
   hearts and the dream resets.
5. Press E for the dream's ability: Hush Bell, Lantern Pulse, Dream Whistle,
   Rewind, or Shadow Swap. In the boss fight, E spends three dawn motes to
   loose a Dawn Flare.
6. Abilities are never required to finish a stage, but each one is the most
   direct answer to its dream's rule.
7. Stars: 1 for clearing, +1 for never losing a heart, +1 for finishing
   under the stage's par time.
8. Keys: WASD / arrows move · Shift tiptoe · E ability · P / Esc pause ·
   M mute. Touch: drag the left stick to move, tap the ability button, tap
   TIPTOE to walk quietly.

■ The five dreams & the family
- The Hush Nursery — Ollie, your little brother, afraid of the dark.
  Hushlings hunt by sound; creaky floorboards wake the whole room. Ring the
  bell to freeze them.
- The Drowned Study — Dad, and the work he never finished.
  The tide rises and ink eels surface. Pulse the lantern to calm the water.
- The Thorn Garden — Biscuit the dog, on a very long way home.
  Briar gates block the path and hounds charge in straight lines. Whistle to
  open the gates and stun the hounds.
- The Clockwork Attic — Mom, and the hour she borrowed.
  Sentinels dash on every tick and pendulums sweep the corridors. Rewind
  four seconds to undo a mistake.
- The Mirror Gallery — June, and your own reflection.
  Your shadow copies where you were seconds ago. Swap places to put it
  behind you.
- The Weaver's Web — the music box at the top of the house.
  A three-phase boss: gather dawn motes, press E to loose the dawn, and bring
  the light up the last staircase.
