    Before midnight

    The music box would not stop

    At midnight, the little music box in the attic began to play on its own. One by one, the Marlowe family fell asleep where they stood — and did not wake.

    Ollie. Dad. Biscuit. Mom. Each one is trapped inside a different nightmare, and the house has grown strange around them.

    You are June. You woke as a small light with a lantern. Gather three lost memories in every dream, wake your family, and climb to the attic before dawn.

    Five dreams, five rules. Every stage asks for three memories and a way out.

    How to play

    The goal

    Find all three memories, then step into the glowing door. Nightmares cost a heart or a guard spark. Lose all light and the dream resets.

    The family

    Every dream you wake adds a rescued soul to your swarm. Each one circles you as a guard spark and absorbs one hit.

    Controls

    WASD or arrow keys — move · Shift — tiptoe · E — dream ability · P / Esc — pause · M — mute

    Touch: drag the left stick to move · tap the action button for the dream ability · tap TIPTOE to walk quietly.

    Five nightmares, five rules

    • Hushlings hunt by sound. Tiptoe past, ring the bell to freeze them.
    • The tide wakes the ink eels. Pulse the lantern to calm the water.
    • Thorn gates block the path. Whistle to open them and stun the hounds.
    • Sentinels dash on every tick. Rewind four seconds to undo a mistake.
    • Your shadow copies you. Swap places to put it behind you.

    Three small secrets

    1. Guard sparks are spent before hearts. Rescue family early and later dreams get kinder.
    2. Par time plus an untouched lantern earns three stars.
    3. The dream ability always has a HUD button with its cooldown.
